## 🧠 Project Overview

**Human Synthesis** is an interactive, scroll-driven digital experience built around the visual transformation of human biological structures into computational forms.

The experience uses procedural 3D geometry, particle systems, neural connections, lighting effects, and synchronized typography to create an immersive visual journey.

As the user scrolls through the experience, different stages of human structure emerge and transform:

**Human Synthesis → Neural Brain → Cellular Structure → Human Bone → Human Skull**

The project was designed as an experimental creative-development piece rather than a conventional landing page.

---

# ✦ Core Experience

### 🧠 Neural Brain

A procedurally generated brain structure is constructed using thousands of particles and organic surface deformation.

The brain incorporates:

- Neural particle systems
- Procedural geometry
- Surface displacement
- Brain folds and sulci
- Glowing particle highlights
- Neural connection lines
- Dynamic scaling and opacity

The brain geometry uses procedural noise to create detailed organic folds and surface variation.

---

### 🦴 Human Bone Structure

The experience transitions from the neural brain into a human skeletal structure.

The bone system includes:

- Particle-based geometry
- Neural-style connections
- Dynamic opacity
- Procedural positioning
- Animated scaling
- Interactive visual transitions

The bone structure appears progressively as the user moves through the scroll sequence.

---

### 💀 Human Skull

The final biological stage introduces a glowing human skull constructed from particles and connected structures.

The skull system includes:

- Procedural particle generation
- Glowing points
- Synapse-style connections
- Dynamic opacity
- Animated scaling
- Computational visual treatment

The skull is designed to feel like a digital reconstruction rather than a traditional 3D model.

---

# 🎞️ Scroll-Driven Storytelling

Instead of using conventional page sections, the scroll position acts as the timeline for the visual narrative.
